## Claims allowed in public editorial content

- N-TET reviews site conditions, sensor roles, interfaces, operating constraints and documentation needs before recommending a configuration.
- N-TET can compare portable, fixed-site and vehicle-mounted equipment for different operating contexts.
- N-TET has public equipment-preparation and testing resources.
- N-TET can prepare technical documents, configuration notes and interface information for buyer review.
- N-TET has a Beijing-based C-UAS team with the experience statement already published on the About page.

## Claims not allowed without new evidence

- Named customer deliveries, acceptance results, overseas deployment counts or project completion dates.
- Certifications, awards, patents, production volume, factory ownership or manufacturing capacity not shown in a verified source.
- Partnerships, memoranda, exhibitions or customer training events without dated source material.
- Founding year, company age, revenue, staff count or market-share claims.

## Editorial boundary

Company articles should explain how N-TET reviews technical questions. They must not be written as fabricated news events. Archive dates are editorial dates, not claims that a delivery, contract or customer event happened on that date.
