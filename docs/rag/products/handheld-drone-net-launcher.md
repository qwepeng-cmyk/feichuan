## Public claims supported for this page

- Handheld physical-capture configuration for trained site-security teams.
- Electronic non-pressurized drive.
- Published net deployment speed: 50 m/s.
- Effective capture distance: 10–20 m.
- Module dimensions: 129 × 75 × 75 mm.
- Module weight: 370 g.
- Net opening: 20 cm.
- Net coverage options: 3.3 × 3.3 m or 5 × 5 m.
- High-strength nylon net cord with a molded nylon housing.
- PWM control with reserved safety timing.
- Single-use module: the net launches outward, does not retract and is not reset for a second launch in the field.

- Multi-device cascade or swarm-response configuration.
- Guaranteed capture rate, universal target coverage or guaranteed site outcomes.
