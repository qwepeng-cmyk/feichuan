Role wording must remain literal:

- Use **exhibited at** only when N-TET had a verified booth or official exhibitor role.
- Use **visited** when team members attended without exhibiting.
- Use **attended** for a conference or meeting without a stronger documented role.
- Use **will attend** for a future plan; do not later present the announcement as proof that attendance occurred.

## 4. Draft structure

- Title with event/activity and accurate N-TET role.
- Fact box: date, venue, role, booth/session, equipment shown.
- Why N-TET attended or performed the activity.
- What was actually shown, checked, discussed, or learned.
- Buyer questions or practical observations that can be published.
- Captioned evidence photos.
- Official third-party source link.
- Relevant product, solution, or contact link.
