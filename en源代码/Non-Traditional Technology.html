About us
Beijing Non-traditional Equipment Technology Co., Ltd. located in Beijing's Lize Business District, is a national high-tech enterprise specializing in unmanned and anti-unmanned systems. Based in China and with a global reach, the company focuses on integrating high-quality resources and markets both domestically and internationally to build efficient and collaborative industrial bridges in non-traditional fields. We are committed to becoming a world-class multinational technology company with international competitiveness. Adhering to the development mission of "connecting technology and applications, and integrating domestic and international markets," we provide top-tier integrated security services to global clients.
