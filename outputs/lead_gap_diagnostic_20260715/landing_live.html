Core challenge

Cannot See, Cannot Identify, Cannot Control

Fast-growing UAV activity and slower management tools create low-altitude blind zones. Site teams first need to see targets, identify events, and keep the handling process under control.

Cannot See

Where is it flying? Who is operating it? Low-altitude targets are hard to perceive across complex sites.

  • Urban blind zones
  • Weak small-target signals
  • Incomplete sensing coverage

Cannot Identify

Authorized flights, unknown targets, and risky activity are difficult to separate in real time.

  • Unclear identity
  • Visual background confusion
  • Weather and signal complexity

Cannot Control

After detection, single-device handling and manual workflows are often not timely, precise, or traceable.

  • Slow response chain
  • Multi-team coordination
  • Traceable records needed
Airports, route operations, key-site security, major events, and urban low-altitude management all need a system-level low-altitude security solution.
